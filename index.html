03 / ABOUT • MULTIDISCIPLINARY CREATIVE

MULTIDISCIPLINARY VISUAL STORYTELLER.

I am a multidisciplinary creative and visual storyteller with a strong foundation in illustration, graphic design, filmmaking, animation and creative direction. My approach to work is driven by conceptual thinking, research, experimentation and attention to detail.

I enjoy transforming ideas into compelling visual narratives across different mediums, whether through illustrations, brand identities, editorial design, advertising, motion graphics or films. I am comfortable working across the creative process, from initial research and ideation to visual development, execution and final delivery.

I have experience in both independent creative work and collaborative projects, including leading teams, developing campaign visuals and directing productions. I am particularly interested in storytelling, visual development and exploring unconventional ways of communicating ideas. Rather than limiting myself to a single discipline, I enjoy adapting to different creative challenges and finding the most effective visual language to bring an idea to life.

Multidisciplinary creative and visual storyteller with a strong foundation in illustration, graphic design, animation, and creative direction. Driven by conceptual thinking, research, and bold experimentation.

Working across brand identities, editorial design, advertising, and film—translating ambitious ideas into compelling visual narratives from initial concept to final delivery.

Akkarakkavu

Set in the mystical landscapes of Kerala, Akkarakkavu is an illustrated continuous scroll story following young Kannan as he wanders deeper into a sacred grove. Guided by a supernatural fox, he is led into a world beyond the familiar—a tale exploring ancient legends and the supernatural charms that he begins to uncover within the mythical grove on the other side of an astral world.
